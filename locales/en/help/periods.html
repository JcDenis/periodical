A period publishes your pending entries by itself, at regular intervals.

Periods
Click a name to edit the period, or the number of entries to see them. Select periods to empty them (entries stay pending) or to delete them.
Publication
Entries are published when a visitor opens the blog home page or a feed. Only entries with the status "Pending" are published. Enable the plugin in the blog settings first.
